    Support¶

    • Email: support@kulplights.com
    • Store and accessories: kulplights.com
    • FPP manual: manual.falconplayer.com

    Before you write, check Troubleshooting. It also lists what to include so we can help quickly.

    Spare parts & accessories¶

    Part Used on
    5 A mini-blade fuses (10-pack) K16, K32, Pro boards, K8-B, K8-B-Scroller
    Pixel connectors (3.5 mm Phoenix-style plugs, screw or spring) all K-series boards (one per port is included)
    Smart Receiver SRx1, long-range SRx2 / SRx4 differential ports
    PocketBeagle 2 / PocketBeagle 2 Industrial PocketBeagle 2 boards
    BeagleBone Black / BeagleBone Green BeagleBone Black boards
    Raspberry Pi 4 K8-Pi, K2-Pi-Servo
    SD card with FPP all boards
    microSD extender, USB extension cable all boards
    Si4713 FM transmitter boards with built-in audio (plugs into the FM socket)
    OLED display all boards

    See the accessories page.

    Returns¶

    New, unopened items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Email support@kulplights.com to arrange it. See the terms and conditions.

    About this manual¶

    This manual is open source. To report a mistake, or suggest an improvement, email support@kulplights.com or use the edit link at the top of any page.
