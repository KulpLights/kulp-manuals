    K16-Pro¶

    16 fused pixel ports with bank switching, 8 two-way differential ports, audio and 2 DMX.

    K16-Pro with a PocketBeagle 2 fitted. Photos may show an earlier board revision.

    This page describes revision v4.1.1 (printed on the board as K16-Pro v4.1.1). Differences in earlier revisions are listed under Board revisions.

    Specifications¶

    K16-Pro
    Local pixel ports 16 (P1–P16), 3-pin 3.5 mm pluggable, plugs included
    Port protection 5 A mini-blade fuse per port
    Power switching per bank of 4, FPP-controlled (v4.1 and later)
    Current monitoring from Falcon V5 receivers only
    Differential ports 8 × RJ45 = 32 strings (17–48), two-way (pair 1/2)
    Total strings 48
    Power inputs 4 terminals rated 30 A, 5–24 V DC, one bank of 4 ports each
    DMX / serial 2 (DMX1, DMX2), RS-485 transmit
    Audio PCM5102A DAC, 3.5 mm line out, FM transmitter socket
    Ethernet 1 × Gigabit (USB-attached)
    USB-A 3
    Display & buttons 128×64 OLED, Up / Down / Enter / Back / Test
    Real-time clock yes, CR1220 backup cell
    Fan header 5 V 4-pin PWM, temperature-controlled; speed monitoring on v4.1 and later
    Expansion header 2×6: I²C + 8 GPIO (Exp.1–Exp.8)
    Size 168.6 × 139.2 mm (6.64 × 5.48 in), 8 mounting holes, 3.0 mm
    Requires PocketBeagle 2 (not PocketBeagle 1), FPP 9.0 or later

    Board map¶

    Power terminals¶

    Terminal Position Feeds FPP reading Also powers the controller?
    V1 left side, upper P1–P4 VIN1 yes
    V2 left side, lower P5–P8 VIN2 no
    V3 right side, upper P9–P12 VIN3 no
    V4 right side, lower P13–P16 VIN4 no

    The blue LED beside V1 lights when it has power. V1 must be powered for the controller to boot.

    See Power for supply sizing, wire size and safety.

    Pixel ports¶

    The sixteen pixel ports are in two rows, eight on each side of the board's centre line:

    Left half Right half
    Top row P1 P2 P3 P4 P9 P10 P11 P12
    Bottom row P5 P6 P7 P8 P13 P14 P15 P16

    Each port is G D V (ground, data, +V), as printed beside it. See Pixel outputs.

    Fuses¶

    Every local port has a 5 A mini-blade fuse next to it. If a port's green LED is dark while the rest of its bank is lit, check that port's fuse first. Never fit a fuse larger than 5 A. See Power → Fuses.

    Bank switching¶

    Each bank of four ports has an electronic power switch that FPP controls, so FPP can turn pixel power off when nothing is playing. See Power → Bank switching.

    Bank switching was added in v4.1. On a v4.0 board every bank is on whenever it has power. See Board revisions.

    Differential ports¶

    17-20 · 21-24 · 25-28 · 29-32 · 33-36 · 37-40 · 41-44 · 45-48

    Each jack carries four strings, numbered as printed above it. They are two-way: Falcon V5 smart receivers report their eFuse status and current back over pair 1/2. Other receivers work as send-only. See Differential ports.

    FPP setup¶

    When FPP detects the K16-Pro it sets itself up automatically. It enables the K16-Pro pixel-string layout, the sensors (input voltages, temperature), the audio output and a disabled DMX output. Out of the box each local port is set to 50 WS2811 pixels at 50 % brightness. Set the real counts and start channels from xLights or the FPP Pixel Strings page. See Configuring outputs.

    Board revisions¶

    The revision is printed on the board next to the model name. FPP also shows it as the Hardware Version under Help → About → Cape Info.

    Revision Changes
    v4.0 Fused ports; banks are always on when powered. Fan speed control.
    v4.1 Adds bank switching (FPP turns each bank of 4 on and off) and fan speed monitoring.

    Dimensions & mounting¶

    • 168.55 × 139.21 mm (6.64 × 5.48 in), 8 plated mounting holes, 3.0 mm, for M2.5 (or #4) screws and standoffs.
    • The mounting holes are connected to ground.
    • Use nylon or metal standoffs at least 6 mm (¼ in) tall, so nothing on the underside touches the enclosure.
    • Mounting drawing: PDF. It is shared by the K16, K16-Pro and K16-Max, which have the same outline and holes.
    • A 3D-printable mount is on Thingiverse.
